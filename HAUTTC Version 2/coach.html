Meet the Coach

Under Coach Pong Mercado's guidance, the HAU Golden Guardians have transitioned into a powerhouse. With dynamic modern coaching strategies, tailored training splits, and pure sports dedication, he ensures player longevity, high tactical IQ, and tournament preparedness.

· Over 15 years of competitive table tennis and coaching experience.

· Led Holy Angel University Table Tennis Varsity Team to multiple regional championships.

· Specializes in peak athletic performance and won multiple titles in his playing years.

Graduate

  • Master of Sports Science.

  • HAU Head Coach: Guided the HAU Varsity Men's Team to Bronze (2024) and the Women's Team to Silver (2024-2025).
